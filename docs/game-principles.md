# Game principles

These principles describe the intended feel and direction of Glow. They are design goals for an evolving game, not a claim that the current CLI implements gameplay.

## The experience

- **A sandbox in an open world.** Give players room to choose their own goals and make their mark on a world that invites exploration.
- **Freedom without a prescribed route.** Avoid funneling players through a fixed campaign or constant instructions. Let curiosity lead; make it easy to discover what can be done.
- **A sense of possibility.** Aim for a world that feels expansive and open-ended. “No limits” is an aspiration for player creativity and exploration, not a promise of literally unlimited content.
- **Meditative by default.** Favor a calm, satisfying rhythm of observing, gathering, building, and improving over pressure and interruption.

## The play loop

- **Gather, grow, adapt.** Make collecting resources, developing capabilities, and responding to a changing world a natural, repeatable loop.
- **Progress through upgrades.** Let players improve gradually, with upgrades that open new options and make prior effort matter.
- **Keep it simple.** Make the basic actions and choices easy to understand. Let depth emerge from how simple systems combine, rather than from layers of menus, currencies, timers, and chores.
- **Avoid mobile-game overcomplication.** Clash of Clans is a cautionary reference for complexity that comes from stacked meta-systems and pressure loops. Add a system only when it strengthens the core experience.

## The creative direction

Glow's reference mix is exploratory sandbox games, automation, open-world action, tower defense, and survival—evoking *No Man's Sky*, *Factorio*, *Vendetta*, tower defense, and *Rust*. These are mood and design references, not a fixed feature checklist. The game should find its own identity by combining the parts that support Glow's principles.

## How Glow is made

- **Agent-developed.** Agents are expected to help design, build, and improve the game, with work kept understandable and reviewable.
- **Always evolving.** Treat the game as a living project. Build in small steps, learn from how systems fit together, and keep the principles as a guide as the world and its possibilities grow.
