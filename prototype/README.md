# Lantern Grove prototype

Open `index.html` in a browser. Move with arrow keys or WASD, or click a destination. Walk over seeds and wood to collect them; spend three seeds and two wood to plant a lantern at your current position. Explore freely and place as many lanterns as the grove's resources allow.

This is a self-contained, client-only design sketch. Its world and progress reset on refresh; it does not implement the planned authoritative game server or shared world.
